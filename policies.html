Reservations

Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand plans change. Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair to both our guests and our small independent team.

More than 48 hours before arrival Full refund issued. No cancellation fee. Cancel directly through your booking platform or contact us at hello@theatlas.com.
24–48 hours before arrival 50% refund of the total booking cost. The remaining 50% will be charged as a cancellation fee.
Less than 24 hours before arrival No refund. The full booking amount will be charged. No-shows are treated as a same-day cancellation.

Non-refundable rates: Some promotional rates and packages are non-refundable at the time of booking. These will be clearly marked before purchase. Please review your booking confirmation for the specific terms that apply to your reservation.

To cancel or modify a reservation, please use your original booking platform or email hello@theatlas.com with your reservation number.

Your Data

Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025

The Atlas Hotel & Lofts ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information we collect
We collect information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, phone number, and payment information — when you make a reservation, contact us, or sign up for our mailing list. We also collect basic usage data when you visit our website (pages visited, browser type, device) through standard web analytics.

How we use your information
We use your information to process reservations, communicate about your stay, send updates and offers you've opted into, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-party services
Reservations processed through Yanolja or other booking platforms are subject to those platforms' privacy policies in addition to ours. We use standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand site usage.

Email communications
If you join our mailing list, you may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send. We will never share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data security
We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Payment processing is handled by PCI-compliant third-party providers — we do not store full card numbers on our servers.

Contact
For any privacy-related questions or to request deletion of your data, contact us at hello@theatlas.com.
