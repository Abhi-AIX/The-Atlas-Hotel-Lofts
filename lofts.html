The Process

Simple From Start
to Move-In

No lengthy applications. No surprise fees. Just a straightforward process to get you settled.

01

Submit an Inquiry

Fill out the form below with your preferred loft type, move-in date, and length of stay. We typically respond within 24 hours to confirm availability and answer your questions.

Ask Us Directly
The minimum stay for all Atlas Lofts is 30 days. After your first month, stays continue on a month-to-month basis with 30 days' notice to vacate. We do not offer nightly or weekly rates through the Lofts — for shorter stays, please visit The Atlas Hotel.
All utilities (electric, water, gas), high-speed Wi-Fi, on-site parking, weekly housekeeping with fresh linens and towels, and all furniture and kitchenware. There are no surprise add-ons. The rate you see is what you pay.
Select lofts welcome pets. Please mention your pet in your inquiry form and we'll confirm which units are available and any applicable pet terms. Service animals are always welcome at no additional charge.
We use a simple short-term residential agreement — not a traditional multi-year lease. The agreement covers your initial stay period and converts to month-to-month after that. Everything is straightforward and signed digitally.
The Atlas Lofts are located at 622 Rivergate Pkwy in Goodletsville — approximately 12 miles north of downtown Nashville, about 20 minutes via I-65. The Grand Ole Opry and Gaylord Opryland Resort are also roughly 20 minutes away. Nashville International Airport is approximately 30 minutes.
Yes — we work directly with companies booking accommodation for employees on assignment. We can invoice companies directly and accommodate multiple employees across different units. Email us at lofts@theatlas.com to discuss corporate rates.
Once your agreement is signed and deposit confirmed, we'll arrange a move-in time with you directly. A member of our team will be on-site for your arrival to walk you through the loft, hand over keys, and make sure everything is exactly as expected.
